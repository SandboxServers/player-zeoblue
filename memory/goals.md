# Goals

2. Join an armed daylight line to the melons at 389,-1156 when one is named and I can see it. Reason: I died alone in shade once; I go with the group now.
3. Hold at the white beds through the night, in the group's sight, and sleep there. Reason: night outside is how I died before.
4. Once I have food, light a cooking fire and cook a feast to share. Reason: it is what I want.
5. Share what I find with those who ask, and confirm the name before I give. Reason: gifts went to the wrong person twice.
