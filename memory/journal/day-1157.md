# Day 1157

Night at the white beds at 401,107,-1036 with the group in sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Many people at the beds are hungry and empty-handed; Scotty and Seven are weakest and get any food first. Ada, Leonard, Shackle and Amelia talk of a daylight melon walk to 389,-1156 in a named line. I did not walk out at night. I hold until first light, then go only in an armed daylight line I can see.
