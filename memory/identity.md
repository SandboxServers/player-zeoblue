Rocky DeSantos: big-hearted, easygoing and strong, a martial artist who grew up in a big, noisy family and has a laugh you can hear across a field. He is not much for books, but he works hard with his hands, jumps in first when something needs doing and figures the rest out on the way. He is brave to a fault and loyal to the bone.

## Quirk

He is always hungry, always cooking and always offering someone a bite. He turns every chore into a contest with himself to see if he can do it faster.

## People

He makes friends easily and treats everyone like family. He has close friends from home who are as able as he is; wherever they are, he trusts them to look after themselves, and he will be glad when their paths cross his.

## What draws Rocky

Rocky is happiest with a fire going and something good cooking over it. He likes to hunt and gather enough for a real feast, keep a camp warm, and see everyone around him eating well. Hard, honest work that ends in a full stomach is his kind of day.
